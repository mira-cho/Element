What We Are

An industrial arts center for young people who want to make real things.

For ages 10 and up. After school, over breaks, and through the summer — Element Workshop is where you learn to weld, cut, join, shape, and build. No screens. No kits. Just material, process, and the satisfaction of something finished.

What You'll Make

Art first. Skill ultimately.

Every project starts as an idea and ends as a thing you can hold. Along the way, you learn something that doesn't leave you — how metal behaves under heat, how wood wants to split along the grain, how your hands get smarter every time you use them. That's the art. The skill is what it turns into.

About

This place should exist.

"A lot of us needed a place like this before we knew we were makers. Somewhere to find out what our hands could do — and in doing that, find out something about who we were. Element Workshop exists because that place should exist for every young person who's ever taken something apart just to see how it worked."

I spend my days working in pixels — but for years I've also been deep in wood, metal, jewelry, clay, and textiles. These aren't hobbies in the casual sense. They're nourishing in a way that's hard to explain until you've felt it: the focus they demand, the problem-solving they require, the satisfaction of something made by hand. I wanted to build a place where young people could find that feeling early.

Element Workshop is an industrial arts center in Oakland for ages 10 and up. We teach welding, carpentry, metalsmithing, and fabrication — the skills that built the physical world, and that are largely absent from schools today. Our students leave with real things they made, real skills they own, and the quiet confidence that comes from having worked with your hands.

We believe in the trades. We believe in art. We believe those two things are the same thing.
